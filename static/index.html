What Jordan Looks For
🚫
No unsubstantiated superlatives
"AI-powered" and "cutting-edge" are not differentiators
👤
Name the customer first
Who exactly is this for — developer, CTO, or line-of-business?
🏷️
Product names must be exact
Wrong names erode credibility with technical readers instantly
📊
Every claim needs a number
Vague directional language without data is unacceptable
🎯
One clear CTA per piece
Tell the reader exactly what to do next — no ambiguity
💬
Lead with benefit, not feature
Start from the customer's outcome, not the technology
